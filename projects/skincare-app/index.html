UI/UX Research · 2025

Skincare App

A self-care app that simplifies skincare routines through personalized guidance, ingredient education, and safer product use.

App DesignObservationsPersonasStoryboarding

A skincare app designed around user needs and UX principles to help people navigate conflicting skincare information, understand the products they own, and build routines tailored to their skin.

the problem

What wasn’t working

With the growing amount of skincare information and products available online, building an effective routine can feel overwhelming. Conflicting advice, complex ingredient lists, and uncertainty about which products work well together make it difficult for people to make informed decisions about their skincare. Many skincare quizzes prioritize product recommendations and sales over users’ individual needs. As a result, people may purchase new products without understanding their purpose, overlook products they already own, or unknowingly combine active ingredients that may irritate their skin. These challenges lead to confusion, unnecessary spending, and inconsistent skincare habits, highlighting the need for a more personalized, transparent, and educational approach to skincare.

the approach

What was the design process

Through 10 contextual observations and interviews, I explored how people approach their skincare routines, where they seek recommendations, and what challenges they encounter. Observing participants as they performed their routines, followed by discussions about their decisions and reasoning, provided valuable insights into their behaviors and needs. The research revealed a need for greater personalization, clearer ingredient information, and better use of existing products. People often relied on online recommendations without knowing whether a product suited their skin or how its active ingredients might interact with the rest of their routine. I synthesized these findings into flow models, personas, storyboards, and affinity diagrams to identify patterns, understand user pain points, and guide the design direction. These insights informed the development of a solution focused on education, personalization, and routine management rather than product promotion.

the outcome

Where it landed

A high-fidelity, working prototype of a skincare app with over 30 screens that helps users personalize their routines based on their skin type and concerns, understand the products they already own and their intended benefits, learn about skincare ingredients and how they work, and follow a structured skincare routine directly within the app. The app design includes a loading animation, an onboarding survey, a step-by-step skincare guide, and a product finder.

key takeaway

What I learned

Understanding users’ habits, frustrations, and goals allowed me to create solutions that addressed real needs rather than assumptions. By prioritizing education and personalization over product promotion, I explored how UX design can make skincare more approachable, informed, and manageable.
