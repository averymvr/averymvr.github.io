UX Design + Front-End Development · 2023

Health-Tech Community Forum

An international health-tech community for an at-home breast cancer screening device.

WebCommunityHealthAccessibility

I led the UX design and front-end development for an international health-tech community forum, where people could ask questions and learn about their at-home breast cancer screening device.

the problem

What wasn’t working

BlueBox, an innovative at-home breast cancer testing device, needed a platform where users can ask questions about the device, share their experiences, and receive credible information directly from the experts.

the approach

What was the design process

Working in a 6-person team using Agile methodologies, I designed and we developed an online forum that allows users to post, favorite, search, and save. We created UML class diagrams and architecture diagrams to refine the system design and ensure alignment with the client. Through multiple design iterations and low-fidelity mockups, I was able to follow the company’s design aesthetic and ensure consistency with the rest of the client’s brand and digital products. The client wanted the forum to have a similar feel to the social media app Pinterest, so I incorporated that into the design by using a 4-column layout with rounded-edge cards. Each card maintained the same width but differed in length depending on the post content. Visual consistency was another key consideration throughout the design, from the overall color palette and styling to smaller details, such as the underline on selected tabs. I also incorporated a linear gradient transitioning from the primary color to the accent color to add visual interest while maintaining a cohesive look and feel. This created a polished and organized layout that aligned with the brand’s style without sacrificing intuitiveness.

the outcome

Where it landed

A complete, responsive forum in React, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and MySQL, with role-based access. I led the UI/UX design and the front-end development, prioritizing ease of use and aesthetic consistency, without sacrificing functionality. The final product featured a login system with role-based access, a social-media-like dashboard, and save, post, and reply functions for customers. Role-based access also gives admins database access to moderate conversations, and tags replies from experts.

key takeaway

What I learned

This project reinforced for me the importance of visual consistency in building a cohesive brand experience, particularly when extending an existing digital product into a new platform.
